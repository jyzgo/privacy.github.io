Privacy Policy

Beatlap: Interval Timer · Adevegames · Effective 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information Beatlap: Interval Timer (com.adevegame.beatlap), published by Adevegames on Google Play, collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version. Beatlap does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or health data, and it has no accounts to sign into. Your workouts and workout history stay on your phone. The only information leaving your device is what the Google AdMob advertising service needs in order to show ads — principally your device's advertising identifier — and, if you choose to buy “Remove ads forever”, the purchase handled by Google Play.

Information collected

DataCollected byWhy
Advertising ID and related device identifiers Google AdMob (embedded in the app) To select and deliver advertisements, to limit how often the same ad is shown, and to detect invalid or fraudulent ad traffic
Coarse technical data sent with ad requests (device model, operating system version, general region derived from IP address, language) Google AdMob To serve ads that are technically compatible with your device
Purchase of “Remove ads forever” (order and payment details) Google Play Billing To process the payment and let the app confirm that you own the purchase. We never see your payment card or Google account details.

This data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • No precise or approximate GPS location
  • No health or fitness data is sent anywhere — Beatlap does not read heart rate, steps or any sensor, and does not connect to Health Connect or other fitness services
  • No contacts, photos, files, messages, calendar or microphone data
  • No user accounts — there is nothing to register for and nothing to sign into
  • No analytics or usage data is sent to us

Data stored on your device

Your workouts, workout history (date, start time, duration and exercise type of each finished session), theme, language and sound settings, and your ad-free status are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are permanently removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data from your device settings. Spoken cues use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — serves the advertisements in the app. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional in-app purchase, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Ad privacy choices — where consent applies, open Beatlap → Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices to change or withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID — Android Settings → Google → Ads. Ads will still appear, but will no longer be linked to previous activity or personalised.
  • No ads at all — watch one rewarded video for 3 ad-free days, or buy “Remove ads forever”.
  • Remove everything — uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored on your device.

Children

Beatlap is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

Data retention and deletion

We operate no servers and hold no database of users, so we have no personal data of yours to retain or delete. Data held by Google in connection with advertising or purchases is retained according to Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google Account privacy controls.

Changes to this policy

If the app begins collecting different information, we will update this page and change the effective date shown at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to adevegame@gmail.com.
